What the assistant does

  • Answers only the FAQs you approved.

When you’re mid-clean, messages wait.

By the time you reply, the client may already have booked someone else — or you spend the evening typing the same answers about prices, availability and what’s included.

How it works

1. You send services, area, hours, price list, 30 FAQs, booking rules and a handoff contact. Access is via OAuth or another safe channel — never passwords in chat.
2. We configure official WhatsApp and your calendar, then run a written test of 30 realistic questions. We do not go live with critical errors (false bookings or invented prices).
3. You accept and go live. We maintain the assistant within the agreed hours. Typical time to service, if materials and provider approvals are complete: about 10 working days — an estimate, not a guarantee.

FAQ

Will this get me more customers?

No, we don’t promise that. We organise the enquiries you already receive and book the standard jobs you already offer.

I already use WhatsApp Business.

If you already reply quickly and you’re happy, you may not need this. It helps when messages pile up while you’re working, or when you want structured booking on a calendar.

What if the AI is wrong?

It can be wrong. We restrict it to your approved text, test before go-live, and pass anything risky to you. It will not invent a price or confirm a booking the calendar did not accept.

Do I have to change my number?

Not automatically. We check whether your current number can be used with the official WhatsApp Business Platform. We never move a number without your written approval.

Is there a sales call?

No. You can try the demo, send your materials in writing, and sign online.

Who are you?

HostBot is run by Cosmin Marian Balint, based in Italy. Work is delivered online. Invoices are issued in Italy.

What about my customers’ data?

You remain the controller of your customer data; we act on your instructions under a written data processing agreement. We use official providers, keep each client’s data separate, and ask only for the minimum needed to quote or book.
